Exam Paper Structure (IFYPH005)

End of Semester 1 Test (EoS1)

90 min · 60 marks · 20%

Compulsory 22-question paper assessing Semester 1 topics (AC1, AC2, AC3, and DC Electricity/Capacitance): 10 × 1-mark MCQs (10m), short-answer & application calculations (30m), and 2 longer structured experimental/multi-part questions (20m). Graph paper provided.

Practical Laboratory Coursework (2 Reports)

0 min · 200 marks · 30%

Two centre-supervised laboratory investigation reports (1200-1500 words each, 100 marks each; e.g. EMF & internal resistance of a cell) assessing experimental planning, uncertainty tables, linear trendline/worst-fit graphing, and evaluation.

IFY Cumulative Final Examination

150 min · 100 marks · 50%

Closed-book cumulative examination covering Semester 1 and Semester 2 (LO1-LO8 / AC1-AC8): 20 × 1-mark MCQs (20m), 7 short-answer questions (15m), 10 application/calculation questions (35m), and 3 longer 10-mark structured questions (30m).

Conventions & Standards

Gravitational Acceleration
g = 9.81 m s^-2 (or 9.81 N kg^-1) close to the Earth, supplied in the NCUK Data, Formulae and Relationships booklet.
Unit Formatting
Negative-index SI notation with single spaces between unit symbols (e.g. m s^-1, m s^-2, kg m^-3, J kg^-1 K^-1, N m^2 kg^-2); table and graph headers written with a solidus (e.g. Potential difference / V, Current / A). Up to 1 mark deducted once per paper if final units are omitted.
Significant Figures
Intermediate working carried out to at least 4 significant figures and final numerical answers reported to 3 significant figures (up to 1 mark deducted once per paper for non-compliance). Error Carried Forward (ECF) awards Method (M) marks while withholding Accuracy (A) marks.
Awarding Body
