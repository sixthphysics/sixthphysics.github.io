Exam Paper Structure (IB-DP-PHYSICS-2025)

Paper 1A (SL): Multiple-Choice Questions

90 min combined (1A+1B) · 25 marks · 20%

Paper 1A and Paper 1B are completed together in one combined 90-minute sitting without interruption (45 marks total, 36% combined weighting). Calculator and 2025 IB Physics Data Booklet allowed; 4 options (A-D), no marks deducted for incorrect answers.

Paper 1B (SL): Data-Based and Experimental Questions

90 min combined (1A+1B) · 20 marks · 16%

Sat together with Paper 1A in one combined 90-minute sitting without interruption (45 marks total). Assesses experimental techniques, uncertainty propagation, error bars, max/min gradients, and graphical data analysis.

Paper 2 (SL): Short-Answer and Extended-Response Questions

90 min · 55 marks · 44%

Compulsory short-answer and extended-response questions on SL core material only across Themes A-E (55 marks total, 90 minutes).

Paper 1A (HL): Multiple-Choice Questions

120 min combined (1A+1B) · 40 marks · 24%

Paper 1A and Paper 1B are completed together in one combined 120-minute sitting without interruption (60 marks total, 36% combined weighting). Covers both SL core and HL/AHL content; calculator and 2025 Data Booklet allowed; no marks deducted for incorrect answers.

Paper 1B (HL): Data-Based and Experimental Questions

120 min combined (1A+1B) · 20 marks · 12%

Sat together with Paper 1A in one combined 120-minute sitting without interruption (60 marks total). Assesses data analysis, uncertainty propagation, power-law/logarithmic linearization, and experimental evaluation.

Paper 2 (HL): Short-Answer and Extended-Response Questions

150 min · 90 marks · 44%

Compulsory short-answer and extended-response questions integrating SL core, HL-only subtopics (A.4, A.5, B.4, D.4, E.2), and AHL extensions (90 marks total, 150 minutes).

Conventions & Standards

Gravitational Acceleration
g = 9.8 m s^-2 (2025 IB Physics Data Booklet printed value; question stems should state g explicitly if 9.81 m s^-2 is used)
Unit Formatting
Negative index SI notation with thin/single space between units (e.g. m s^-1, kg m^-3, J kg^-1 K^-1, W m^-2); solidus (/) avoided.
Significant Figures
Final numerical answers match the least precise input datum (typically 2 or 3 significant figures); absolute uncertainties rounded to 1 significant figure.
Awarding Body
International Baccalaureate Organization (IBO) (First Teaching August 2023 / First Assessment May 2025)
