Exam Paper Structure (AQA-7407-7408)

AS Paper 1 (7407/1): Core Year 12 Structured Questions

90 min · 70 marks · 50%

Assesses Sections 1-5 (3.1-3.5) via short and long structured questions (70 marks). Standalone AS qualification.

AS Paper 2 (7407/2): Practical Skills, Data Analysis & Year 12 MCQs

90 min · 70 marks · 50%

Section A: 20 marks practical/data analysis; Section B: 20 marks synoptic structured; Section C: 30 multiple-choice questions (1 mark each) across Sections 1-5.

A-Level Paper 1 (7408/1): Sections 1-5 and 6.1 (Periodic Motion)

120 min · 85 marks · 34%

60 marks of short and long structured questions plus 25 multiple-choice questions (1 mark each, 4 options A-D) covering Year 12 core + Circular Motion & SHM.

A-Level Paper 2 (7408/2): Sections 6.2 (Thermal), 7 (Fields) and 8 (Nuclear)

120 min · 85 marks · 34%

60 marks of short and long structured questions plus 25 multiple-choice questions (1 mark each); assumes synoptic knowledge of Sections 1-6.1.

A-Level Paper 3 (7408/3): Practical Skills, Data Analysis & Option Topic

120 min · 80 marks · 32%

Section A (45 marks): compulsory practical experiments, uncertainty analysis, and graph linearization; Section B (35 marks): one chosen option from Sections 9-13.

Conventions & Standards

Gravitational Acceleration
g = 9.81 m s^-2 (printed in AQA 7408, OCR H556, and CIE 9702 Data and Formulae Booklets to 3 significant figures)
Unit Formatting
SI negative-index notation (e.g. m s^-1, kg m^-3, N kg^-1, J K^-1, Ω m); eV, MeV, u, kW h, ly, and pc permitted where specified.
Significant Figures
Minimum 40% mathematical demand (DfE Level 2+ maths including logarithms, exponentials, trigonometry, and vector geometry). Final answers quoted to the minimum significant figures of supplied data (typically 2 or 3 s.f.).
Awarding Body
AQA (with DfE Core, OCR Physics A H556 & Cambridge CIE 9702 overlap notes) (GCE Reformed Linear Specification (Current / 7408))

Frequently Asked Questions

Which UK A-Level Physics exam boards does SixthPhysics cover?

Questions are organized by the DfE GCE AS/A-Level Physics core and AQA 7407/7408 (Sections 1–8 plus Options 9–13: Astrophysics, Medical Physics, Engineering Physics, Turning Points, and Electronics), with extensive overlap across OCR A (H556), Edexcel, and CIE 9702 core topics.

Can I separate Year 12 (AS) from Year 13 A-Level topics?

Yes. Use the Y12 and Y13 level filter in the Practice Desk, Mock Exam builder, or PDF Paper Maker to focus exclusively on AS Year 12 foundations or full A-Level Year 13 content such as Further Mechanics, Thermal Physics, Fields, and Nuclear Physics.

What gravitational field strength value is used for A-Level questions?

All UK A-Level Physics calculations and solver-verified mark schemes follow the standard g = 9.81 m s⁻² convention.
